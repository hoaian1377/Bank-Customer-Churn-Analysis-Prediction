## Key Insights

1. Customer inactivity is strongly associated with higher churn.
2. Germany has a significantly higher churn rate than France and Spain.
3. Middle-aged customers show higher churn rates than younger groups.
4. Certain product-usage segments have substantially higher churn.
5. Early-tenure customers represent an important retention opportunity.

## Recommendations

- Launch re-engagement campaigns for inactive customers.
- Investigate customer experience in Germany.
- Develop targeted retention strategies for high-risk age groups.
- Review product bundles and customer-product fit.
